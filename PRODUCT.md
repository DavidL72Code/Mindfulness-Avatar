# Mindfulness Connected

## Platform

Responsive web application with a companion mobile implementation. This design pass applies to the web application only.

## Users

Primary users are adults—especially caregivers and people under everyday stress—who want a short, approachable mindfulness practice without having to understand meditation terminology. This is a working assumption based on the current practice library and product copy.

## Purpose

Help a person recognize what they need, begin the right guided practice quickly, and build a sustainable mindfulness habit over time.

## Positioning

Mindfulness Connected is a practical, personal guide rather than a content catalog or clinical tool. It combines short guided sessions, a conversational avatar, mood reflection, and visible progress in one calm experience.

## Operating context

Users may arrive distracted, tired, anxious, or short on time. The interface must reduce decisions, remain understandable at a glance, and work from narrow mobile browser widths through large desktop displays.

## Core capabilities and constraints

- Preserve the twelve-practice library and constellation identity.
- Preserve the AI mini guide, text and voice interaction, session playback, progress, profile, authentication, and localization.
- Make one recommended next action obvious without hiding the wider library.
- Never imply medical diagnosis or guaranteed health outcomes.
- Support keyboard navigation, reduced motion, strong focus states, readable contrast, and translated text expansion.

## Brand commitments

- Warm and encouraging, never childish or patronizing.
- Calm without becoming beige, generic, or visually empty.
- Progress should feel supportive rather than competitive.
- Constellations are a navigation and memory device, not astrology advice.
- Motion should explain state, reward completion, or direct attention.

## Evidence

- Current repository, practice content, localization bundles, session flows, and product history supplied by the user.
- Competitive references named by the user: Duolingo, Quizlet, Amazon, and leading mindfulness products.
- Product interviews are still open; audience emphasis and priority order above are inferred working assumptions.

## Product principles

1. The next useful action is visible within seconds.
2. Organize practices around a person’s need, not internal content structure.
3. Reveal depth progressively; do not crowd the first viewport.
4. Use progress to invite return, not create guilt.
5. Keep the guide present and available without letting it obstruct the product.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All controls need accessible names, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets where practical, semantic structure, and non-motion alternatives.

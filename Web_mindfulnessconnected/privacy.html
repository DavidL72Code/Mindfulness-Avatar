Your information should feel cared for, too.

This policy explains what Mindfulness Connected collects, why we use it, the services that help operate the app, and the choices available to you.

Effective August 11, 2026

At a glance

We use account and activity information to provide your personalized mindfulness experience. We do not sell your personal information. You can permanently delete your account and account-linked data from the mobile app or request help by email.

Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you create or update an account, we may collect your email address, first and last name, optional date of birth, password credentials handled by Firebase Authentication, and language preference.

Mindfulness activity

We store session history and progress information such as completed practices, duration, active days, streaks, and aggregate statistics. Mood check-ins may be stored locally on your device.

Guide conversations and voice

Text you send to the mindfulness guide is processed to generate a response. If you choose push-to-talk, microphone access and speech recognition are used to turn your speech into text. Generated voice audio is used to speak guide responses. Do not include information you do not want processed by these services.

Technical information

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical data such as IP address, device or browser type, request timestamps, and error or security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

How we use information

  • Authenticate accounts and keep your profile and progress available across signed-in devices.
  • Provide sessions, mood check-ins, personalized greetings, activity statistics, guide responses, and generated speech.
  • Respond to support and account-deletion requests.
  • Maintain reliability, prevent abuse, diagnose errors, and improve app safety and performance.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the app. These may include Google Firebase for authentication and database storage, Google Gemini for AI-generated guide responses, Render for backend hosting, Vercel for website hosting, and speech or text-to-speech services for voice features. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers only as needed to provide, secure, and maintain the app, or when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Retention and deletion

Account profile and session information is kept while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. A non-profile deletion marker containing the former Firebase user identifier may be retained to prevent still-valid authentication tokens from recreating deleted data. Some security, backup, or legal records may also remain for a limited period where required or technically necessary.

Requesting deletion sends a secure, expiring confirmation link to the email associated with your account. Nothing is deleted until you confirm from that link. Confirmed deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, profile document, stored session history, and activity statistics. Mood check-ins stored locally can be removed by clearing or uninstalling the app. Learn how on the public account deletion page.

Your choices

  • Review or update profile information in the app.
  • Decline microphone permission and type messages instead.
  • Sign out without deleting your account.
  • Delete your account in the mobile app or request deletion assistance.

Security, children, and wellness

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Mindfulness Connected is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

The guide supports general wellness and is not medical care, diagnosis, or emergency support. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or an appropriate crisis resource immediately.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will post the revised policy here and update its effective date. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@mindfulnessconnected.app.

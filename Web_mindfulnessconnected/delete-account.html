Mindfulness Connected Privacy policy

Delete your account and associated data.

Request a secure deletion link from the mobile app. Nothing is deleted until you open the email and confirm on the protected page.

Last updated August 11, 2026

Delete in the mobile app

  1. Open Mindfulness Connected and sign in.
  2. Open Profile, then choose Delete account.
  3. Choose Send deletion confirmation email.
  4. Open the link sent to your account email. The link expires after 30 minutes.
  5. Review the deletion details, then choose Permanently delete my account.

What will be deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile information.
  • Your stored session history, completion data, streaks, and activity statistics.

Mood check-ins are stored locally rather than in the account database. Clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app to remove them from the device.

This action cannot be undone. Limited security, legal, or backup records may be retained when required by law or for a short technical retention period.

Cannot access the app?

Email a deletion request from the address associated with your account. Include the words “account deletion request.” We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the request.
